# Historical legal documents

The files in this directory and the related `translations/` directory are
preserved for traceability and qualified legal review. They are not current
protocol specifications, do not establish the production status of any IPI
service, and must not be treated as legal advice.

These documents are excluded from the repository's Apache-2.0 license and from
the CC0-1.0 dedication that applies to `ipi/`. They remain **all rights
reserved** unless an individual file explicitly states otherwise.

Do not publish a modified version as an operative policy or agreement without
an explicit legal and governance review. Proposed replacements should identify
the affected service, jurisdiction, controller or contracting entity,
effective date, version, translation authority, and approval record.
